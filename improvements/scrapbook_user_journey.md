# User Journey: The "Project Scrapbook" in Action

**The Scenario**: You are renovating your Guest Bathroom. It's a mess of ideas, measurements, and shopping trips. Here is how the **Project Scrapbook** changes your life.

---

## Phase 1: The "Spark" (Inspiration)
*You are browsing Instagram on your couch and see the perfect blue tile.*

1.  **Action**: You tap "Share" on the Instagram post and select **Tooltopia**.
2.  **App**: Tooltopia opens a small popup: "Add to which project?"
3.  **Action**: You tap "New Project" -> "Guest Bath".
4.  **Result**: The photo is saved to your Scrapbook. AI auto-tags it: `#tile #blue #inspiration`.

---

## Phase 2: The "Reality Check" (Planning & Measuring)
*You are standing in the bathroom with a tape measure. Your hands are dusty.*

1.  **Action**: You open the "Guest Bath" project. You see the tile photo there.
2.  **Action**: You tap the **Orange "+" FAB** and choose **Microphone**.
3.  **Voice Note**: "The vanity wall is 1.8 meters wide, but there's a pipe 30cm from the left."
4.  **Result**: A "Voice Card" appears.
    *   *Visual*: A waveform.
    *   *Smart Text*: AI transcribes it and highlights **"1.8 meters"** and **"Pipe: 30cm from left"**.
5.  **Action**: You tap "+" again and choose **Camera**. You snap a photo of the ugly old sink to show the plumber later.

---

## Phase 3: The "Hardware Store Run" (Shopping)
*You are at the store. You see a vanity on sale for $400.*

1.  **Action**: You open the Scrapbook.
2.  **Action**: You look at the **Voice Card** from Phase 2. "1.8 meters wide".
3.  **Decision**: The vanity is 1.2 meters. It fits!
4.  **Action**: You tap "+" -> **Camera**. You snap the price tag ($400).
5.  **App**: AI reads the price tag.
    *   *Card created*: Photo of Vanity.
    *   *Metadata*: "Price: $400".
    *   *Budget Update*: The "Estimated Cost" bar at the top of the screen jumps up by $400.

---

## Phase 4: The "Execution" (Doing the Work)
*You are tiling the wall. You forgot how to mix the grout.*

1.  **Action**: You tap "+" -> **Link**. You paste a YouTube video "How to Mix Grout for Beginners".
2.  **Result**: A video card appears. You can play it directly in the Scrapbook while you work.
3.  **Action**: You realize you're running out of spacers.
4.  **Action**: You tap "+" -> **Note**. "Buy more 2mm spacers".
5.  **App**: This note is automatically highlighted yellow because it detects an "Action Item".

---

## The Result
Instead of a folder full of receipts and a brain full of worries, you have a **Visual Dashboard**:
*   [Photo] Blue Tile Inspiration
*   [Voice] "Wall is 1.8m"
*   [Photo] Vanity ($400)
*   [Video] How to Grout
*   [Note] "Buy spacers"

**It's not just a list. It's your project brain.**
