# MyProjects: The "DIY Companion" Vision

## The Hard Truth: Is it useful right now?
**Honestly? Barely.**
Right now, "MyProjects" is just a digital filing cabinet. It's a form where I type in things I already know. As a DIYer, if I have to do data entry, I'll just use the Notes app on my phone or a piece of paper.
**To be useful, the app needs to do work FOR me, not just store work I've already done.**

## What a DIYer Actually Needs (The "Pain Points")

When I'm doing a project (e.g., "Renovating the Guest Bathroom"), here is my reality:
1.  **I'm scattered**: I have screenshots of tiles on my phone, a link to a YouTube tutorial, a napkin sketch of measurements, and a paint chip in my wallet.
2.  **I'm anxious**: "Will this cost $500 or $2,000?", "Can I actually do plumbing?", "What if I forget a crucial part and have to drive back to the store?"
3.  **I'm hands-full**: I'm on a ladder, my hands are dirty, and I need to check a measurement *now*.

## The "Killer Features" (How to fix it)

### 1. The "Project Scrapbook" (Stop being a Form, Start being a Workspace)
Instead of a rigid form (Name, Date, Status), give me a **visual workspace**.
*   **"Quick Dump"**: Let me paste a URL, upload a photo of a price tag, or voice-memo a measurement ("Wall A is 2.4 meters").
*   **Inspiration Board**: Let me pin photos of finished projects I like.
*   **Why?**: This replaces my scattered screenshots and mental notes. It becomes the *one place* I look.

### 2. The "Reality Check" (Budget & Feasibility)
I don't want to just track what I spent. I want to know if I *should* do the project.
*   **The "DIY vs. Pro" Calculator**: I enter "Tiling 10sqm". You tell me: "DIY Cost: $300 + 2 weekends. Pro Cost: $1,200." -> **This is huge value.**
*   **Hidden Costs Estimator**: I list "Tiles". You remind me: "Don't forget grout, spacers, a tile cutter, and sealant." (The app knows I need these).

### 3. The "Hardware Store Mode" (The Shopping Companion)
When I walk into the hardware store, the app should change.
*   **One Master List**: Don't make me open 3 different projects. Aggregated shopping list sorted by aisle (Plumbing, Lumber, Paint).
*   **"Do I have this?"**: I'm staring at a $50 specialized wrench. The app should tell me: "You already have a 10mm socket set in your inventory, you don't need this." OR "You don't have a tile cutter, rent one here."

### 4. "Project Recipes" (Don't make me start from scratch)
90% of DIYers are doing the same 20 projects (Painting a room, Building a deck, Installing a shelf).
*   **Pre-loaded Templates**: I click "Paint a Room".
    *   *Auto-generated Task List*: (Prep walls, Tape trim, Prime, Paint).
    *   *Auto-generated Shopping List*: (Roller, Tray, Tape, Drop cloth).
    *   *Auto-generated Tool List*: (Ladder, Screwdriver for removing outlet covers).
*   **Why?**: This saves me 30 minutes of planning and prevents the "I forgot the masking tape" disaster.

## Summary of Changes
| Feature | Current App | The "DIY Companion" App |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Entry** | Manual forms | Voice notes, photo uploads, templates |
| **Planning** | Empty text box | "Recipes" with pre-filled steps & materials |
| **Shopping** | List of text | Aggregated list + "Check Inventory" alert |
| **Value** | Digital Notebook | Project Manager & Expert Consultant |

**Verdict**: If we can't offer templates or smart inventory integration, we should consider **removing the generic "Projects" CRUD** and focusing entirely on the "Tool Inventory" aspect, because a generic list isn't better than Apple Notes. But if we add **Templates** and **Shopping Intelligence**, it becomes indispensable.
